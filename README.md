## What it does

- **Animated kick guides.** Watch a figure perform the front kick, roundhouse, side kick, back kick and axe kick one step at a time, plus jab and cross, squat, lunge and plank. Pause, go back, or slow it down.
- **40-minute daily training.** No gym tools, only your body and things at home. About 70% of every session is legs: kicks, jumps, strength and hip flexibility. The rest is stance, punches, core and grip.
- **Made for your body.** Enter your height and weight first, then the app builds your plan.
- **Streak tracker.** Train every day and keep your streak alive.
- **Height and weight log.** Track your growth and weight every few weeks, with simple healthy-growth habits.
- **AI coach.** Chat with a personal coach powered by Google Gemini, using your own API key.
- **Motivation.** Serious, personal messages that turn doubt into discipline.
- **Works offline.** Install it on your phone like a normal app.

## Install on your phone

- **Android (Chrome):** open the app link, tap the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the app link, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## AI coach setup

1. Get a free API key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/).
2. In the app, open **Profile** and paste the key.

Your key stays on your own device. It is never uploaded or shared.

## Safety

This app gives general fitness information and is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before you start, especially if you are still growing or plan to use supplements. Stop if you feel pain or dizziness. No app can guarantee changes in height, weight or strength.
